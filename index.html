Privacy policy

Highway Swing is published by dmelk. This policy explains how information is handled in the 0.6.1 closed-test release.

Effective: 10 October 2026
Android package: com.dmelk.highwayswing

Data in the game

The game stores preferences, saved progress and scores locally on your device so they remain available between sessions. This includes sound settings and your personal best. The game does not send this information to dmelk.

This release has no online accounts, developer-operated cloud saves, game analytics, live advertising or in-app purchases. Roadside billboards show local game artwork; they do not request advertisements or track ad impressions.

When you contact us

If you email dmelkk@gmail.com, we receive your email address, any sender name, your message and any attachments you choose to include. We use this information only to respond to your request and resolve the issue you report.

Our email provider processes correspondence to deliver and store messages. We keep support information only as long as needed to respond to and resolve your request. We do not sell personal information or use support messages for advertising.

Storage, security and deletion

Local saves remain in the app's storage until you remove them. You can delete them by clearing Highway Swing's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the game. Your operating system's backup settings may retain or restore app data; manage those backups through your device or account settings.

Local information is protected by your device's app-storage controls. Support correspondence uses our email provider's security controls and is accessed to handle your request. To request access to, correction of or deletion of support information you sent us, email dmelkk@gmail.com.

Google Play and external websites

Google Play distributes the game. Google and your operating system may process information for downloads, updates, security, diagnostics or backups under their own settings and policies. That processing is separate from the game's local saves. See Google's privacy policy.

Links you choose to open use your browser or email app. Those services and the destination websites follow their own privacy policies. This policy page has no scripts, analytics, tracking pixels or external fonts. Its hosting provider may process technical request information, such as an IP address, to serve the page. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Audience

Highway Swing is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, contact us so we can delete it.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy before introducing advertising, analytics, purchases or other features that change these data practices, and provide any required notices and choices. The effective date above identifies this version.
